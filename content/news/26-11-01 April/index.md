This week, Naveen Kumar and Nick Smoliak from the DeepNano group James Watt School of Engineering, University of Glasgow presented their seed funding projects at the APRIL AI Hub summit in Shefileld.

Naveen Kumar told us how to make rainbows in a chip and Nick Smoliak presented his work on how to simulate and model resistive memories using AI and atomistic simulations. 

Thank you, everyone, from APRIL AI Hub for the excellent organisation and great programme. Looking forward to attending next year's meeting.
